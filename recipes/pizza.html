Pizza Recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 grams all-purpose flour
  • 7 grams instant yeast (1 sachet)
  • 1 teaspoons sugar
  • 1.5 teaspoons salt
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 300 milliliters warm water
  • 400 grams crushed tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 0.5 teaspoons salt
  • 0.5 teaspoons sugar
  • 300 grams mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 60 grams Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 10 fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (for drizzling)

Steps in making the Pizza

  1. Make the dough: In a large bowl, mix 500 grams all-purpose flour, 7 grams instant yeast (1 sachet), 1 teaspoons sugar, and 1.5 teaspoons salt.
    Add 2 tablespoons olive oil and 300 milliliters warm water and mix until a shaggy dough forms.
    Knead on a floured surface for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.

  2. Let the dough rise: Place dough in a lightly oiled bowl,
    cover with a damp cloth, and let rise in a warm place for 60 minutes
    60:00 until doubled in size.

  3. Make the tomato sauce: Heat 1 tablespoons olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat.
    Sauté 3 garlic cloves, minced for 15 minutes 15:00. Add 400 grams crushed tomatoes,
    1 teaspoons dried oregano, 0.5 teaspoons salt, and 0.5 teaspoons sugar.
    Simmer for 15 minutes until slightly thickened. Set aside to cool.

  4. Shape the dough: Preheat oven to 250°C (480°F).
    Punch down the dough and divide into 2 balls.
    On a floured surface, stretch or roll each ball into a thin round, about 30 cm wide.
    Transfer to a lined or oiled baking tray.

  5. Top the pizza: Spread a generous layer of tomato sauce over each base, leaving a 2 cm border for the crust.
    Scatter 300 grams mozzarella cheese, shredded evenly, then sprinkle 60 grams Parmesan cheese, grated on top.
    Drizzle with 2 tablespoons olive oil (for drizzling).

  6. Bake: Bake for 10–12 minutes
    12:00 until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly and lightly browned.
    Scatter 10 fresh basil leaves on top right after removing from the oven.
