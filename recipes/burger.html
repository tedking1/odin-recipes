Burger Recipe

Ingredients

  • 600 grams ground beef (80/20)
  • 4 burger buns
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoons salt
  • 0.5 teaspoons black pepper
  • 0.5 teaspoons smoked paprika
  • 4 cheddar cheese slices
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 2 large tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 dill pickles, sliced
  • 4 tablespoons ketchup
  • 4 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons mustard
  • 1 tablespoons butter

Steps in making the burger

  1. Make the patties: In a bowl, combine 600 grams ground beef (80/20), 1 small onion, grated, 2 garlic cloves, minced, 1 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce, 1 teaspoons salt, 0.5 teaspoons black pepper, and 0.5 teaspoons smoked paprika
    .Mix gently — don't overwork the meat. Divide into 4 equal portions and shape into patties about 1.5 cm thick.
    Press a small indent in the center of each to prevent puffing.

  2. Chill the patties: Place patties on a plate, cover, and refrigerate for 15 minutes 15:00
    to help them hold their shape while cooking.

  3. Cook the patties: Heat a skillet or grill over high heat. Cook patties for 3–8 minutes
    08:00 per side for medium doneness.
    In the last minute, place a slice of 4 cheddar cheese slices on each patty and cover to melt.

  4. Toast the buns: Melt 1 tablespoons butter in a pan over medium heat.
    Place 4 burger buns cut-side down and toast for 1–1m 30s 01:30 until golden.

  5. Assemble: Spread 4 tablespoons mayonnaise on the bottom bun and 2 tablespoons mustard on the top.
    Place 4 lettuce leaves, then the cheesy patty, then 2 large tomatoes, sliced,
    1 red onion, thinly sliced, 4 dill pickles, sliced, and a drizzle of 4 tablespoons ketchup.
    Cap with the top bun and serve immediately.
